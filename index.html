Measurements & Condition

A Label Can Lie. A Tape Measure Can't.

Every serious thrift shop lists real flat measurements next to the label size. So does this one:

  • Coat / Jacket / Hoodie: Chest (in), Length (in), Shoulder
  • Trousers: Waist (in), Length / Inseam (in)
  • Condition grade: Excellent / Good / Fair — any flaw gets its own photo
  • Carpet / Bedsheet: Size (ft), with a Shop Pickup tag on large pieces

On out-of-town delivery, honestly: couriers take 4–5 days to reach Gilgit and Skardu, and 5–7 days to remote parts of Gilgit-Baltistan, and winter road closures can add delays. So no delivery date is promised — you get a dispatch date plus a tracking number instead.

Note: This first listing is built from the winter stock described by the shop — coats, jackets, hoodies, trousers, ladies' long coats, kids' coats / high-necks / frocks, carpets, curtains and bedsheets. Real photos and prices from each new drop will be updated in this same format. Final availability is always confirmed at the shop: every piece is one of a kind, so we confirm it's still available when you message.

Common Questions

Everything You Might Ask

I like a piece but I live outside Gahkuch. What happens?

Pickup comes first. For Gilgit / Yasin, the piece is sent by vehicle or courier after full advance payment. There is no cash-on-delivery for a one-of-one piece sent to another town — if it comes back, the buyer who wanted it that same day is gone too.

I reserved a piece, but someone else paid first. Then what?

First to pay holds the right — the rule is written on this page. If a piece turns out to be sold after your payment, your full amount is refunded.

What if the size turns out wrong?

That is exactly why every piece lists real measurements (chest, length, waist in inches) next to the label size. And with shop pickup, you can hold the piece, try it on, and then decide — the biggest advantage of landa.

How often does new stock arrive?

A new drop lands every 3 days: 10–15 new photographs per category, uploaded as one fixed drop. Ads promote that drop and the whole shop — never a single piece.

The Reserve System • Rules in Writing

How to Reserve

Four simple steps and the house rules in writing — adapted for Gahkuch with a half-advance hold, the shop's own plan.

1

Browse

Open a category page and check the photo, price, real measurements (chest / length / waist) and condition grade. Never trust the label size alone — the tape measure is the truth.

2

Reserve or Buy

Every piece has two buttons: Reserve — free hold, you get a reserve code to show at the shop. Buy Now — opens the payment choices: pay the full price on WhatsApp and the piece is yours, or pay half on WhatsApp and the piece is held 1–2 days in your name, the rest on pickup.

3

Hold Time

Gahkuch locals: free hold until the shop closes today, pickup tomorrow. Need 1–2 days? A half advance on WhatsApp is required.

4

Pickup / Delivery

Shop pickup is the default. For Gilgit / Yasin, pieces are sent by vehicle or courier only against full advance, with tracking.

The House Rules — So There Is Never an Argument

  • First to pay wins. Looking at a piece or asking about it does not reserve it.
  • The half advance is non-refundable if a 1–2 day hold expires without pickup — this is stated clearly before any money is taken.
  • A screenshot does not reserve a piece. A long hold is confirmed only when the JazzCash / EasyPaisa amount is visible in the shop's own account, and you receive the code plus the expiry time in writing.
  • Time up = back on sale. An unpaid reservation expires on its own.
  • If a piece sells out after your payment, the full amount is refunded.
  • Large home pieces (carpets and similar) are Local Pickup only.

Only 1 Piece • No Restock

Your Reserve Code

Show this code at the shop. For Gahkuch locals, this piece is held for you until the shop closes today. To keep it 1–2 days, a half advance is required — otherwise the piece returns to sale when the time expires.

A long hold is confirmed only when the payment is visible in the shop's account — not on a screenshot. If the piece has already sold, your payment is refunded in full.
